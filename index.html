About

Engineering starts with understanding.

I am a software engineering undergraduate, full-stack developer, and professional freelancer based in Colombo, Sri Lanka. I work with clients locally and internationally to turn real-world requirements into useful, dependable digital experiences.

I do not begin with a framework, a feature list, or a visual trend. I begin by studying the people, process, limitations, and intended result. That approach helps me choose technology because it fits the problem—not because it is fashionable.

My work spans full-stack web development, custom software, user-interface design, business automation, and solution architecture. I am especially interested in technology that reduces repetitive effort, improves access to information, and enables better decisions.

My approach

Real problems before digital products.

Every valuable solution begins with evidence: what is happening now, who it affects, and what a better outcome should look like.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Study the real environment, repeated work, delays, errors, and unmet needs.

  2. 02

    Understand

    Identify the root cause, users, constraints, risks, and desired business result.

  3. 03

    Design

    Choose the simplest suitable workflow, technology, and experience for the problem.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Build, validate, document, launch, and improve based on actual use and feedback.
