03

Working method

Problem-first engineering

A solution is valuable only when it changes something that matters.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Study the reality

    Repeated work, delays, errors, unmet needs, and the people affected.
  2. 02

    Understand

    Find the root cause

    Users, constraints, existing tools, risks, and the intended result.
  3. 03

    Design

    Choose the right response

    The simplest suitable workflow, technology, and user experience.
  4. 04

    Deliver

    Build and validate

    Implement, test, document, launch, measure, and improve.
