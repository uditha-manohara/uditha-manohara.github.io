About

Software engineering starts with understanding.

I am a Software Engineer and Founder & CEO of Neziora, based in Colombo, Sri Lanka. I also work as a professional freelancer and am currently completing a BSc (Hons) in Software Engineering while building useful, dependable digital systems.

I do not begin with a framework, a feature list, or a visual trend. I begin by studying the people, process, limitations and intended result. That approach helps me choose technology because it fits the problem, not because it is fashionable.

My work spans full-stack web development, custom software, user-interface design, business automation and solution architecture. I am especially interested in technology that reduces repetitive effort, improves access to information and enables better decisions.
